VCAT Human-Powered Rover – Seating Position Tool

Adjust dimensions to lay out a recumbent pilot and read off joint angles & coordinates for CAD.

Inputs (all in mm or degrees)

Rider segments
Seat / torso
Back angle is from horizontal, leaning backwards (0° = fully flat, 30–45° typical recumbent).
Crank / pedal circle (relative to hip)
Handlebar (hand target, relative to hip)
Coordinates are given with hip at (0, 0), X forward towards the crank, Y up. You can drop these directly into CAD sketches or param tables.
